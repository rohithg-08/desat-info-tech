ERP & production management

One system from raw material to dispatch.

We build ERP that understands how things are made, not only how they are sold. Your stores, production floor, quality team and accounts all work from the same live data.

Without a connected systemWith a connected ERP

Scattered records

Stock in one sheet, orders on WhatsApp, production on paper. No one sees the full picture.

One source of truth

Sales, purchase, stock and production share the same records, updated as work happens.

Shortages found too late

Material runs out in the middle of a job, and the delivery date slips.

Planned production

Bills of material and work orders check stock first and raise purchase requests before the line stops.

Reports built by hand

Days go into collecting numbers at month end, and they are already old when read.

Reports as it happens

Output, rejections, pending orders and GST data are ready on a dashboard whenever you need them.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Can the ERP be customised for our production process?

Yes. We study how your shop floor works first, then set up the bill of material, work orders, quality checks and reports to match it.

Can we bring our existing data from Excel?

Yes. We import items, customers, suppliers and opening stock from your spreadsheets so you don't start from zero.

Can staff use it on the shop floor?

Yes. Production entries and stock checks work on a phone or tablet, so your team can update records at the machine.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Manufacturers, shops, service providers, schools, clinics and start-ups. If your work involves repeated manual steps or scattered records, we can help.

Do you support us after launch?

Yes. We train your team, stay available for changes and keep improving the system as your business grows.
