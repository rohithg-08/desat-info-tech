What services does Desat Info Tech provide? +

Desat Info Tech is a software development company focused on delivering innovative digital solutions. We specialize in custom software development, web applications, mobile apps, AI solutions, cloud services, UI/UX design, and digital transformation for businesses ready to grow.

Who owns the intellectual property (IP) and code? +

You retain 100% full intellectual property and source code ownership from day one. All repositories, cloud assets, design files, and documentation belong completely to your company upon milestone completion.

How fast can you build and launch our software product? +

For MVP products and focused web or mobile apps, we typically design, build, and launch within 4 to 6 weeks. For comprehensive enterprise software platforms, we deliver working increments in 2-week agile sprints.

Can you integrate AI solutions into our existing software? +

Yes. We integrate modern AI capabilities—including Generative AI, custom LLMs, document understanding, intelligent recommendation engines, and automated workflow agents—into your existing web, mobile, and backend systems.

Do you offer post-launch maintenance and cloud support? +

Yes. We provide ongoing product maintenance, cloud infrastructure management (AWS, Azure, GCP), security patching, 24/7 uptime monitoring, and continuous feature sprints to support your long-term growth.

Can you work alongside our existing in-house developers? +

Absolutely. We can augment your internal engineering team with dedicated developers, lead specific architecture initiatives, or manage the end-to-end product lifecycle from design through production.
